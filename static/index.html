Pair this phone

One time only. After this, opening the remote connects automatically.

Step 1, once: trust this laptop. Download the certificate and tap Allow. Then open Settings → Profile Downloaded → Install. Then Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings → turn on “Phone Remote CA”. Reload this page: the “Not Private” warning should be gone.
Tip: pair from the Home Screen app, not a Safari tab. In Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen, open “Laptop Remote” from there, then pair. Safari tabs and Home Screen apps keep separate logins.
